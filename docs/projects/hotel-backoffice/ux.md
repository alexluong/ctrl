## 1. Personas

Three people, not three logins: the same person can wear the owner hat and the setup hat. Roles are capability bundles (product.md §2); the app shows the same Front Desk to everyone and simply hides what you cannot do.

### 1.1 Receptionist — "Linh", the desk, 24/7 in shifts

Sits at the desk in the lobby. Phone rings, guests walk in, housekeeping shouts room numbers down the stairs. She is on the app all day, in short bursts of 10–60 seconds between guests. Vietnamese UI; English when a foreign guest is looking over her shoulder. Shares the role with two colleagues but has her own login (every event carries who did it).

**A day**

- 06:30 shift starts. Opens the **room map**: who is in, who is leaving today, what is dirty. Housekeeping starts on the departing rooms.
- 08:00–11:00 departures. Guest comes down: open the stay from the map, glance at the bill, take the money (cash, transfer, sometimes card via the bank machine), check out. Room flips to dirty.
- 11:00–14:00 phone bookings for next week; a company books 6 rooms for a training course (group booking, rooms decided later).
- 14:00–20:00 arrivals. Walk-ins: pick a free clean room on the map, type the guest, check in. Booked guests: find the booking, assign the room if not yet, check in with ID names.
- Through the day: minibar, laundry, a taxi — posted to the guest's bill from the stay page. Desk buys a gas cylinder for the kitchen: recorded as an expense.
- 22:30 handover. Counts the cash for the owner (manual today; see product.md §8 Later).

**Needs from the UI**: two clicks from map to money. Never leave the room map far behind. Big readable states (free / in / dirty / OOO). Error text in plain Vietnamese saying what to do next.

### 1.2 Owner / manager — "Anh Tuấn", runs the hotel, not the desk

Owns the building, is at the hotel most days but not at the desk. Wants to know today's picture in one glance and to be the one person who can undo money (void, refund, write off). Signs in on a laptop in the office or on the phone in the evening. Also the person the desk hands the cash to.

**A day**

- Morning: opens the app, wants **today**: occupancy, arrivals, departures, cash taken yesterday, what companies owe. (Today this is spread over room map, calendar, receivables; a dashboard is still a gap, §6.)
- Any time: a receptionist calls: "I posted laundry to the wrong room". Owner opens the stay and voids the line, or moves it.
- Month end: opens **receivables**, calls the companies that owe, records their transfers, writes off the one that was dissolved.
- Reviews **expenses** by category for the month.
- Occasionally checks a room's or stay's **history** to see who did what.

**Needs from the UI**: the same Front Desk as the desk (so he can cover a shift), plus an unmistakable "owner only" feel on the dangerous buttons. Numbers must add up to the cash in his hand.

### 1.3 Setup admin — the owner on onboarding day (and a few times a year)

Same person as 1.2, different hat, different rhythm: a long sitting once at go-live, then a few minutes at a time when a season changes or a receptionist joins or leaves.

**Sessions**

- Go-live: room types → rooms per floor → rates for the coming months → charge categories (standard list) → companies the hotel already bills → staff accounts and roles.
- New season: add a rate range for each room type.
- Staff change: create an account, add to staff as receptionist; later mark as former staff.
- Room renovation: take out of order for weeks (that is a desk action too), or retire the room.

**Needs from the UI**: forms that refuse nonsense loudly (overlapping rates, a room type in use), and "retire, never delete" so history stays readable.

### 1.4 Footnote: system operator (Alex / dev)

Not a hotel role. Creates the tenant and first owner via the admin SDK, watches the event log and projection tables in the **System** console (`/system`), runs the night cron. Sees every hotel. Never appears in the hotel's own nav for hotel users; listed here only so the IA is complete.

## 2. Journeys

Each step: **screen** → control → `Command` (product.md §11). Steps with no command are reads. "Today" = what the built app does on 2026-09-24 (origin/main); gaps are collected in §6.

### 2.1 Receptionist

**J1 Walk-in, one guest, pays and leaves** (the core loop; slice 1–3)

|---|---|---|---|

**S1 Go-live**

| # | screen | does | command |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Setup → Room types | Deluxe sleeps 2, Family sleeps 4 | `DefineRoomType` |
| 2 | Room map → Define a room | 101…305 with floor and type | `DefineRoom` |

**S4 Room changes** — Setup → Room types → **Retire** (refused while a room uses it); Room map → room → **Take out of order** for a renovation.

## 3. Information architecture

### 3.1 Apps → nav → screens (target)

product.md §2 says three apps. The built app has one flat nav; §6 lists the difference. Target nav, left to right, is grouped so the desk's three screens come first:

```
[ Front Desk ]              [ Back Office ]              [ Setup ]        [ System ]
 Room map   /                Receivables  /receivables    Setup /setup     /system…
 Calendar   /calendar        Expenses     /expenses       Accounts /accounts   (operator only)
 Bookings   /bookings        People       /guests
   New booking               Dashboard    (5.3 in progress)
   Booking   /bookings/:id   Reports      (5.3)
   Stay      /stays/:id
   Room      /rooms/:id
```

### 3.2 Who sees what

| screen | route | receptionist | owner | notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sign in | `/sign-in` | ✓ | ✓ | username + password; no sign-up |
| Room map | `/` | ✓ | ✓ | home |
| Room | `/rooms/:id` | ✓ | ✓ | hk state, OOO, note, history |
| Calendar | `/calendar` | ✓ | ✓ | rooms × nights; unassigned list |
| Bookings | `/bookings` | ✓ | ✓ | list + new booking form |
| Booking | `/bookings/:id` | ✓ | ✓ | stays, group's bill, finish, history |
| Stay | `/stays/:id` | ✓ | ✓ | guests, nights, room, bill, routing, history |
| People | `/guests`, `/guests/:id` | ✓ view | ✓ + erase | guests and contacts |
| Receivables | `/receivables` | ✓ record payment | ✓ + write off | by company |
| Expenses | `/expenses` | ✓ record | ✓ + void | by category / period |
| Accounts | `/accounts`, `/accounts/:id` | – | ✓ | logins; reset password |
| Setup | `/setup` | – | ✓ | room types, rates, categories, companies, staff |
| Dashboard, Reports | – | – | ✓ | in progress (5.3) |
| System | `/system…` | – | – | operator |

Owner-only screens render a "this is the owner's" page for a receptionist rather than 404, so the desk learns where things live.

### 3.3 Navigation rules of thumb

- Room map is home; every screen has a way back to it in one click.
- A stay is the hub of money: the bill lives on the stay page, the group's bill on the booking page; there is no separate "folio" screen.
- History sits at the bottom of every entity page (room, stay, booking, person, account), same shape everywhere: version · event · details · when · who.
- Owner-only controls are shown greyed with the hint "owner's", not hidden, so the desk knows to call.

"Built" notes say what origin/main does on 2026-09-24 when it differs. Every entity page ends with the same **History** table (version · event · details · when · who), drawn once in 4.9.
